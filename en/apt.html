A Pirate's Tale

Caribbean Sea, end of the 17th century

Infamous Cap’n Roger hid away the greatest treasure of all Seven Seas on a secret island.

17 years later, Roger’s island is finally found again by a pirate crew, closely tied to the events of the past. Following their dreams, they find themselves in a drama that only starts to unfold when a strange, young woman gets washed up on the beach.

This tale follows the paths of scurrilous scoundrels, dutiful marines and a brave native tribe, each chapter told from a different point of view, tying together the pieces of old Cap’n Roger’s legacy.
