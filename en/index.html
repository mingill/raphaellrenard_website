The Author

"A good story writes itself."

Raphael L. Renard

Raphael L. Renard is a scrivener, a thread-connector, a typist.

But the true author of all good stories is life. As fictitious and mythical as they might be, there is always a bit of truth in all of them. From experiences of the world in which we live, to deeds of the heroes and villains who slumber deep within us, to everyday things that we all know and that make us who and what we are.
